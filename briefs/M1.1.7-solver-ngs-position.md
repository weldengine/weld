## Context

Eighth core sub-milestone of the M1.1 rigid arc (M1.1.0–15, `PhysicsModule` freeze at M1.1.15). Delivers the **NGS position solver** — the position-correction answer the spec mandates for the rigid branch (`engine-physics-forge.md` §1.1, §1.7, §1.7.2) and the **first consumer** of the per-point `penetration` the M1.1.6 constraints carry unused.

M1.1.6 is a velocity solver with **no positional bias of any kind** (no Baumgarte, no split impulse); its accepted interim artifact is a resting body whose penetration is bounded at its at-impact value and never resorbed. M1.1.7 removes that artifact. The plan's acceptance criterion is stacking stability (`engine-phase-1-plan.md`, M1.1.7 line: "Empilement de boxes stable (pas de jitter/enfoncement)").

The design is the Jolt lineage (`ContactConstraintManager::sSolvePositionConstraint`, `AxisConstraintPart::SolvePositionConstraint`, `PhysicsSystem::JobSolvePositionConstraints`), cross-checked against Box2D's position solver. Pseudo-impulses are integrated straight into the pose and the velocity change is discarded (Catto, GDC 2007 — the Baumgarte term is split out of the velocity solver precisely so it adds no momentum). The KB spec section §1.7 was authored and re-uploaded **before** this brief; it is normative for everything below.

## Scope

- **`SolverConfig` relocation + additive fields (E1).** `SolverConfig` moves from `rigid/velocity_solver.zig` to a new `rigid/solver_config.zig`: one struct shared by two solvers belongs in neither. `velocity_solver.zig` imports it; `rigid/root.zig` re-exports from the new owner. Call sites use `rigid.SolverConfig` and are untouched. Additive fields, spec §1.7.2 table: `position_iterations: u32 = 3`, `penetration_slop: Real = 0.005`, `position_correction_factor: Real = 0.2`, `max_penetration_correction: Real = 0.2`. Existing fields, defaults and semantics unchanged (`velocity_iterations = 8`, `restitution_threshold = 1.0`). Entry validation on the position path, same debug-assert discipline as M1.1.6's `restitution_threshold`: all four finite, `penetration_slop >= 0`, `max_penetration_correction >= 0`, `position_correction_factor` in `[0, 1]` (a factor above 1 over-relaxes and can diverge).
- **Surface anchors in `prepare` (E1).** `ConstraintPoint` gains `local_anchor_a: Vec3r` and `local_anchor_b: Vec3r`; `ContactConstraint` gains `local_inv_inertia_a: Mat3r` and `local_inv_inertia_b: Mat3r`. `prepare` reconstructs the two surface points from the manifold point and its penetration — `surface_a = position + 0.5 * penetration * n`, `surface_b = position - 0.5 * penetration * n` (exact: the manifold point IS the midpoint of the two surface points, `pipeline/narrowphase/manifold.zig` `ContactPoint` doc + the clipping and point-core producers) — and stores each in its own body's local frame (`conj(q) * (surface - x)`). `local_inv_inertia_*` is copied from `MotionProperties.local_inv_inertia` (pose-invariant), so the position pass never calls `motionProperties` in its inner loop. The existing world lever arms `r_a`/`r_b`, effective masses and `v_n⁻` capture are untouched — the velocity solver's inputs do not change. `penetration`'s doc comment updates: consumed here, at `prepare`, to derive the anchors; never re-read from the frozen manifold during the iterations.
- **Pose mutators on `BodyManager` (E1).** Additive `setPosition(id, Vec3r)` / `setRotation(id, Quatr)`, stale-safe (`isValid` guard), mirroring the M1.1.5 velocity setters. **INTERNAL** to `forge_3d` — like `addTorque`/`setAngularVelocity`, they are not part of the day-1 `PhysicsModule` surface; flagged for the M1.1.15 freeze review.
- **`rigid/position_solver.zig` (E2).** New sibling file. `solvePositionRange(bm: *BodyManager, constraints: []ContactConstraint, from: usize, to: usize, cfg: SolverConfig) PositionSolveResult`. Same range-shaped form as `solveRange` — the M1.1.8 island seam (contract (d)). Import discipline identical to the sibling files: `foundation`, `weld_forge`, `../config.zig`, `../body_manager.zig`, `contact_constraint.zig`, `solver_config.zig`. Never `broadphase.zig`, never any `pipeline/` file.
  - **Normal FIXED in world for the whole pass.** `c.normal` is read as-is and never re-rotated. Normative reason (spec §1.7.2): the frozen manifold does not carry the reference-face owner, and that owner is **not** necessarily A — `manifold.zig` selects it by alignment with the contact axis (`a_is_ref = align_a >= align_b`; for a sphere against a box the reference is the box). Attaching the normal to a body would require extending the frozen `ContactManifold`.
  - **Per iteration, per point, in the velocity solver's order** (constraints ascending by pair key as `build` sorted them, points in manifold order): world anchors from the CURRENT poses; `sep = max((p_b - p_a) · n + penetration_slop, -max_penetration_correction)`; `if (sep >= 0) continue`; lever arms from the midpoint of the two current anchors (`p = 0.5 * (p_a + p_b)`); world inverse inertia rebuilt from the **current** rotation (`R * local_inv_inertia * Rᵀ`) per body; `lambda = -position_correction_factor * sep / k(n, r_a, r_b)` with `k` the normal effective mass at the current geometry; `P = lambda * n` applied immediately to both poses (Gauss-Seidel). The clamp is on the **error** in, not on the correction out (Jolt `mMaxPenetrationDistance` semantics); the `sep >= 0` gate replaces any upper clamp.
  - **Orientation:** `dtheta = ±I⁻¹ * (r × P)` applied by the first-order linearized rule `q <- normalize(q + 0.5 * (dtheta_quat ⊗ q))`, `dtheta` world-space on the LEFT — the same rule as `integratePositions`. No `|dtheta|` division, therefore no absolute guard (M1.1.4 threshold discipline). Deliberate divergence from Jolt's exact axis-angle `SubRotationStep`, which needs an absolute `|dtheta| > 1e-6` guard because that routine is shared with kinematic pose driving (large deltas) — a regime absent from a position solver, where the angular delta is bounded by `max_penetration_correction` over the lever arm. Do NOT factor a shared helper with `pipeline/integration.zig`: `rigid/` must not import `pipeline/` outside the narrowphase facade, and the integrator has a flagged additive option (exact non-linearized orientation integration) the position solver would not follow.
  - **True-zero pose-write guards.** Static and kinematic bodies zero the correction naturally (`inv_mass == 0`, `local_inv_inertia == 0`) — no `body_type` branch. The pose write is nevertheless guarded at EXACT zero (position delta and rotation delta each strictly non-zero) because `normalize` on an already-unit quaternion is not bit-neutral: a non-dynamic body must come out **bit-unchanged**.
  - **Termination.** An iteration that applies no correction at all ends the pass (Jolt semantics: `if (!applied_impulse) break`). No convergence-threshold constant of any kind.
  - **`PositionSolveResult { iterations_run: u32, min_separation: ?Real }`.** `min_separation` is `null` when no point was evaluated. Telemetry only — never a control input. Feeds `get_solver_iterations_stats` (`PhysicsDebugProvider`, spec §1.9); no other consumer is claimed at M1.1.7.
- **Cycle insertion (E3).** The test harness's normative per-tick cycle gains step (6) between `integratePositions` and `storeContacts`, per spec §1.7: `... (5) integratePositions -> (6) solvePositionRange -> (7) storeContacts + endTick -> (8) proxy updates`. The harness header's normative cycle comment is updated to the spec's numbering. Production `step()` stays M1.1.15.
- **M1.1.6 acceptance-test tightening (E3).** `tests/solver_test.zig`, `test "box dropped on a static ground comes to rest without sinking (e = 0)"`: the "penetration bounded by its at-impact value, absolute non-recovery accepted" statement is now obsolete. Replace it by a **recovery** statement — resting penetration `<= penetration_slop` — and tighten the envelope to `y_final` in `(1.0 - 2 * penetration_slop, 1.0 + 1e-4)` (analytic rest is `ground_top 0.5 + box_half 0.5 = 1.0`; NGS converges to `1.0 - penetration_slop` from below). RED-first: write the tightened assertion before the solver exists and watch it fail. **If the converged value falls outside that envelope, that is a STOP requiring analysis — never a widened envelope.** Any other existing test whose envelope must move is reported in its gate verdict with the measured before/after, never silently adjusted.
- **Additivity seam pin (E3).** `position_iterations = 0` performs no correction whatsoever (early return) and therefore reproduces the M1.1.6 resting behaviour exactly — penetration bounded at its at-impact value, unrecovered. Pinned by a dedicated test, so the M1.1.6 contract stays observable after M1.1.7.
- **Acceptance suite (E4).** New `forge_3d/tests/position_solver_test.zig`, pinned in `forge_3d/root.zig`'s comptime block (no `build.zig` change — `zig build test-forge-3d` picks it up through the module).

## Out of scope

- Island manager, sleep detection, parallel solving (M1.1.8). The range-shaped entry and the per-range termination are the seams; nothing island-aware is built here. **Documented consequence:** at M1.1.8 each island range terminates independently, which changes the aggregate result versus one global range — that is the intended semantics (Jolt iterates position steps per island) and a global termination would break per-island equivalence.
- Joints (M1.1.16–18). Jolt's per-island position-step count raised by constraints is an additive seam, not built here.
- `PhysicsWorld` / `step()` orchestration / `PhysicsModule` instantiation / ECS Transform sync (M1.1.15).
- Raycast / shapecast / overlap / point queries (M1.1.9–10). CCD / speculative contacts.
- Shapes beyond sphere/box/capsule (M1.1.11+). `forge_2d`.
- Per-shape / per-face `PhysicsMaterial` implementation (precedence already recorded and now specified in §4; implementation lands with per-shape materials).
- **The M1.1.4 clip-recentring precision boundary — examined and NOT absorbed here.** `sep` is a difference of two nearly-equal world points, so its absolute noise is `~floatEps(Real) * |x|`: at f32 and 10 km that is ~1.2 mm against a 5 mm slop (margin ~4x, positive). This is the same structural worldspace limit the narrowphase already documents and answers with the f64 comptime flag (spec §1.5, §1.7.2) — not a new class. It is **characterized in the spec and pinned by a test** (far-from-origin stack, below); the clip-recentring fix stays a future precision hotfix upstream in `generateManifold`.
- Any reintroduction of a positional bias in the velocity solver. A Baumgarte term or split impulse found in `velocity_solver.zig` is a **STOP** (contract (a)).
- Benchmarks. Absolute perf targets land with the full `step` (M1.1.3/4/5/6 precedent).

## Notes

- **Inherited M1.1.6 contracts, binding on this design.** (a) The velocity solver carries no positional bias — reintroducing one is a STOP. (b) Contact points come from `collidePair` (canonical `BodyId` order, frame-stable `feature_id`), never from bare `collide`. (c) `SolverConfig` extension is additive. (d) `solveRange`'s range shape is the island seam; the position entry mirrors it. (e) The resting-penetration artifact is what this milestone removes. (f) The per-tick cycle is normative in the spec, not guessable. (g) Candidate pairs are retained while the fat AABBs overlap; the harness keeps every emitted pair (a conservative superset). (h) The tangent basis is discontinuous at a dominant-axis flip — never assume basis stability.
- **Design points verified against the reference implementations** (read, not recalled): normal fixed in world for the whole pass (`ContactConstraintManager.cpp`, `ws_normal` read once before the point loop); two distinct body-local anchors with `separation = (p2 - p1) · normal`, no initial-penetration term; world inverse inertia rebuilt inside the point loop from the current rotation (`Body::GetInverseInertia` = `GetInverseInertiaForRotation(current rotation)`); lever arms from the midpoint of the two current anchors; clamp on the error in, `if (separation < 0)` gate; termination on "no impulse applied", per island. Box2D's position solver was the cross-check; where the two diverge, Jolt is followed (the project's declared physics reference), with the exception noted below.
- **Alternatives examined and rejected.** Normal attached to body A (the frozen manifold does not carry the reference-face owner and it is often B — this was corrected during scoping). Frozen world inverse inertia during the pass (unjustified approximation; the pass rotates the bodies it reads). Two identical midpoint anchors plus a stored `penetration_0` scalar (algebraically within `O(theta * penetration)` of the two-anchor form, but it stores an extra scalar and is not the reference discretization). Box2D's `min_separation >= -3 * slop` termination (introduces a magic multiplier; Jolt's "no impulse applied" needs no constant). Box2D's clamp on the correction rather than on the error. Jolt's exact axis-angle rotation step (needs an absolute `1e-6` guard, imported for a regime that does not exist here). A slop derived from a scale — body-size-derived makes the resting overlap pair-dependent (non-uniform rest offset in a stack), contact-`coordScale`-derived makes it world-position-dependent. Jolt's `0.02` slop (coupled to its speculative-contact distance, which is out of scope — the number without its reason). A separate `PositionSolverConfig` struct (M1.1.6 named `velocity_iterations` explicitly for a `position_iterations` sibling in the same struct).
- **Threshold discipline (M1.1.4).** `penetration_slop` and `max_penetration_correction` are absolute lengths in metres and that is **assumed and motivated**: they are physical tolerances of the internal unit scale (`engine-units.md`), of the same class as M1.1.6's `restitution_threshold`, and they classify nothing and guard no numerical degeneracy. Every other guard in this milestone fires at true zero. No new geometric epsilon anywhere.
- **Determinism by construction (anticipates M1.1.14).** Iteration order identical to the velocity solver (constraints ascending by pair key, points in manifold order); no hash container on the path; every constant named in `SolverConfig`; the termination decision is a pure function of the range's state.
- **Spec status.** `engine-physics-forge.md` §1.7 / §1.7.2 / §4 / §1.9 were authored and re-uploaded to the KB **before** this brief and are normative. Any deviation discovered in implementation is a Recorded deviation here and triggers a corrective re-upload at close (Claude.ai-produced) — never a silent divergence.
- **No deferred debt.** Any finding surfaced at any point in this milestone is fixed inside this milestone. One milestone, one Claude Code session.

- **`solvePositionRange(bm, constraints, from, to, cfg) PositionSolveResult`** implemented exactly as §1.7.2 prescribes: normal read from the constraint and never re-rotated; per iteration, per point, in the velocity pass's order — world anchors from the current poses, `sep = max((p_b − p_a)·n + slop, −max_penetration_correction)`, `sep >= 0` gate, lever arms from the midpoint of the two current anchors, world inverse inertia rebuilt from the CURRENT rotation, `lambda = −factor·sep / k`, `P = lambda·n` applied immediately to both poses (Gauss-Seidel). Entry calls `solver_config.assertPositionDomain`. `position_iterations == 0` early-returns before any evaluation (`iterations_run = 0`, `min_separation = null`).
- **Orientation** by the local `rotateByDelta` — `q ← normalize(q + ½·(Δθ_quat ⊗ q))`, Δθ world-space on the LEFT, no `|Δθ|` division and therefore no absolute guard. Not factored with `pipeline/integration.zig` (import boundary + the integrator's flagged additive exact-orientation option).
- **True-zero pose-write guards**: the position delta and the rotation delta are each written only when strictly non-zero, so a static/kinematic body — whose correction is exactly zeroed by `inv_mass == 0` and a zero local inverse inertia — never goes through `normalize` and comes out bit-unchanged. The same predicate defines "applied", so the no-correction termination cannot be fooled by a zero-magnitude correction (it also makes `position_correction_factor = 0` terminate after one iteration instead of spinning).
- **`min_separation`** is the raw geometric `(p_b − p_a)·n`, before the slop and before the error clamp — the worst residual penetration, telemetry only (Box2D tracks the same quantity; Jolt tracks none).
- **Measured** (temporary diagnostics, removed before commit): the converging two-sphere case settles at `separation = −0.00511760` against a 5 mm slop — a 1.2e-4 residue, matching the `(1 − factor)ⁿ` contraction and inside the named `convergence_residue = 1e-3`. The current-rotation inertia oracle reproduces the implementation **bit-for-bit** (`|observed − expected| = 0`), while the frozen-inertia oracle is off by 5.3e-3 on its worst quaternion component — 5× the discrimination guard, and identical at f32 and f64, so the gap is geometric, not float noise. The extra post-`prepare` rotation was widened 0.4 → 0.9 rad to buy that margin.

#### Finding — the five-box stack creeps laterally at the default iteration budget

| `velocity_iterations` | `position_iterations` | lateral | speed | top `y` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 0 | 0.075 | 0.0002 | 4.883 (no recovery — M1.1.6) |
| 8 | 1 | 0.086 | 0.068 | 4.967 |
| 8 | **3 (default)** | 0.131 | 0.067 | 4.971 |
| 8 | 8 | 0.298 | 0.072 | 4.972 |
| 16 | 3 | 0.022 | **0.000** | 4.984 |
| 64 | 3 | 0.002 | 0.000 | 4.998 |

Reading: the chain depth is what triggers it (1, 2 and 3-box stacks stop dead — 2e-6, 5e-4 and 4e-3 m of lateral offset with zero residual speed). Gauss-Seidel visits the four points of a face manifold in sequence, so each position pass leaves a small residual tilt; the velocity pass at 8 iterations does not re-equilibrate a five-deep chain, and the tilt walks the stack. ### RD-1 — the resting-penetration acceptance criterion is unreachable as frozen (E3)

**Frozen text** (Scope, "M1.1.6 acceptance-test tightening"): *"Replace it by a **recovery** statement — resting penetration `<= penetration_slop`"*.

**Why it cannot hold.** The `sep >= 0` gate makes `penetration == penetration_slop` the FIXED POINT of the pass, and the pass is a geometric contraction toward it (`separation + slop` is multiplied by `1 − position_correction_factor` per correction). A contraction never crosses its fixed point, so the resting penetration approaches the slop **from above** and reaches it only in the limit — never in finitely many floating-point steps. The bare inequality is a boundary the model cannot attain; it is a property of the model, not of the test.

**Form retained.** `resting_penetration <= penetration_slop + 16·floatEps(Real)·coord_scale` — the M1.1.4 noise class, same `k = 16` as the narrowphase's accumulated-rounding band, `coord_scale = 1 m` (the contact's height). A TEST tolerance only: no epsilon of any kind was added to production code, and the imposed `y_final ∈ (1.0 − 2·slop, 1.0 + 1e-4)` envelope is untouched and met with a 5 mm margin.

**Measured.** f32: `resting_penetration = 0.00500059`, `y_final = 0.99499947` — an excess of 5.9e-7 over the slop, ≈ 5 ULP of the 1 m contact coordinate, and 3.2× inside the named margin. f64: `0.00500000` / `0.99500000` — the excess is below the printed 1e-8 resolution. The physical content of the criterion is intact: the ≈ 7 cm at-impact penetration is resorbed to 5 mm, a factor of ~14.

**Also stronger than asked.** The penetration is measured on the MANIFOLD (`ContactPoint.penetration` of the resting constraint), not re-derived from the anchors — it observes what the narrowphase reports about the physical overlap rather than what the solver's own state says.

**Spec follow-up at close.** `engine-physics-forge.md` §1.7.2 needs one line: the attainable resting bound is `penetration_slop + O(floatEps·coordScale)`, not `penetration_slop` — the slop is the fixed point, approached from above. This belongs with the §1.7 KB re-upload (Claude.ai-produced, Guy re-uploads).
