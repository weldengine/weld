## Context

A **sensor** is a body that detects without responding. The corpus has declared `TriggerEnter` and `TriggerExit` since its first physics draft and specifies `CollisionShape.is_trigger` as the authoring surface, yet nothing in the interface can express the role and nothing in the engine reads it: `BroadphaseLayer.trigger` exists with no producer and no consumer, exactly the situation `BodyType.kinematic` was in before M1.1.12. This milestone closes that chain.

It delivers the **lower half** of the capability, and the boundary is deliberate. The engine produces an **overlap state** and two **deltas**; the translation into typed events on the Tier 0 bus belongs to M1.1.15 with the rest of the orchestration, because `forge_3d` depends only on `foundation/math` and the public component types of `src/modules/forge/api/` — a C1.1 exit metric — and the bus lives on `World`, which the solver cannot reach. The state, not the event stream, is the source of truth: the Tier 0 bus drops its oldest entry on saturation, so an ownership set reconstructed from the flow would be wrong on the first saturation.

Two fields land on `BodyDescriptor` here. This is the **second-to-last window**: after M1.1.15 freezes `PhysicsModule`, neither could land at all, and without the first, no caller can declare a trigger.

## Scope

- **`BodyDescriptor.is_trigger` and `BodyDescriptor.trigger_layer_mask`**, transcribed from `engine-tier-interfaces.md` §1 at version **0.9** field for field, name for name, default for default. No interface function is added: the §12 count stays at 27, and a reviewer expecting a new entry should read `engine-physics-solver.md` §1.13.12 for why there is none.
- **The role is a property of the INSTANCE, never of the geometry.** The shape store is shared and a `ShapeId` is referenceable by several bodies, so the field cannot sit on `ShapeDescriptor` without forcing two bodies that share a sphere to share their nature (§1.13.1). A mixed body does not exist: an entity that must both collide and detect carries **two bodies**.
- **`CollisionShape` gains `trigger_layer_mask`** as the ECS authoring source of the descriptor field, and its pinned layout moves from 48 to **52 bytes**. Both the comptime assert and the test that doubles it are updated together; a pin changed in one place only is the defect this bullet exists to prevent.
- **`BodyFlags` carries `is_trigger`** on one of its five reserved bits — no size change. The mask is a **separate column** of the body store, not a flag.
- **Broad-class assignment, and it is this milestone's by prior decision.** M1.1.1's scope boundary assigned the trigger/debris layer-assignment sources here by name. Fixed priority: `is_trigger` first, then body type — `static` to `static`, **everything else to `dynamic`**. A kinematic body therefore lands in `dynamic`, deliberately: the class names what *moves*, not what is *simulated*.
- **The `trigger` row and column of `default_layer_pairs` go to `false` in full.** This **reverses a cell asserted at M1.1.1** — `dynamic × trigger` was `true`, and `broadphase_test.zig` asserts that pair positively today. The reversal is a revision of an M1.1.1 decision, recorded as such and not slipped in: killing the pair at the source is less work than filtering it downstream every tick, and it is a stronger guarantee, since a trigger that never reaches step 4 cannot be forgotten by a downstream filter.
- **Detection and physical response are two axes with no shared mechanism** (§1.13.2). `is_trigger` removes the response; the mask governs what is seen. A trigger can detect a `static`, `dynamic` or `debris` body and another trigger, and none of that produces a contact. Making the broad class carry a gameplay policy is the error this bullet forbids.
- **A dedicated sensor traversal, and it is structurally required rather than merely preferable.** `computePairs` is **moved-driven** — it enumerates proxies that left their fat AABB since the last call — so it returns a delta and never a snapshot: two motionless bodies overlapping for a hundred ticks do not appear in it. The pass enumerates the **trigger proxies** and descends the trees and the unbounded lists for each one's candidates. That direction and not the reverse: triggers are rare against bodies, and the set is indexed by them.
- **The mask is unilateral.** It belongs to the trigger and describes what *it* sees. Two overlapping triggers are two relations evaluated separately, and A may see B without B seeing A. Same mechanism and same semantics as the object-layer mask of the eight query entries (§1.11.5), which is the only object-layer filtering the engine owns — there is **no object-layer matrix**, and none is built here.
- **Detection is exact, never AABB.** A candidate retained by the mask is confirmed by the narrowphase under the conditions of §1.11.12: accepted as soon as the GJK regime is not `separated`. The tree descent bounds the candidates; the AABB never decides membership.
- **No threshold of its own, and no hysteresis.** The boundary is the GJK contact margin (§3), not a second margin invented for sensors. A body resting exactly on a trigger's surface, inside that few-ULP band, may flip between ticks. That is accepted and stated in §1.13.6: a hysteresis would need a length-dimensioned, therefore tuned, therefore scale-dependent threshold, which §1.11.2's discipline forbids on this path.
- **Three exclusions from the physical response** (§1.13.7). No constraint, no impulse, no island membership — no pair reaches step 4. No wake cause: a detection is not a solicitation under §1.8.4 and is not a fifth cause beside W1–W4. And the **character controller ignores trigger bodies by construction, in all three of its collection paths** — sweeps, depenetration, ground probe.
- **The controller exclusion is by construction and not by mask, and the distinction is load-bearing.** All three of `character.zig`'s collectors read `collisionLayer` and test it against a mask; none consults the broad class. Left alone, a trigger would stop blocking rigid bodies while still blocking the player — an invisible wall for the player alone. The controller's mask is over **object** layers and a trigger can live on any of them, so requiring the game to reserve one for triggers would rebuild in mirror the confusion of §1.13.2. « No physical response » is the definition of the role; a controller sweep *is* a physical response.
- **The eight query entries, by contrast, keep seeing triggers.** They traverse every broad class (§1.11.1 point 3), editor selection and debug overlays depend on it, and a caller that wants none has its own mask. The symmetry with the controller would be an error here, and it is tested in **both** directions so that nobody restores it by reflex.
- **The observable state is a set of ORIENTED ENTITY PAIRS**, never body handles. Four normative rules: several body overlaps between the same two entities aggregate to **one** pair, whose exit fires only when the **last** of them disappears; a pair whose two members are the same entity is dropped; the pair is oriented from the trigger, so two mutually detecting triggers produce **two** pairs; and the current set is **rebuilt in full every tick**.
- **Full rebuild is what makes handle recycling harmless**, and this is a property of the reconstruction rather than of the type. No body handle appears in the state and no membership is carried forward, so a reissued `BodyId` inherits nothing. State held by increments would lose that immunity — say so where the state is declared, or a later optimisation will silently break it.
- **Two deltas: `entered` and `exited`**, the two set differences. There is no third list — §1.13.12 states why `TriggerStay` does not exist in either the 3D or the 2D corpus, where both do carry a `CollisionStay`.
- **The pass is filtered by no sleep state whatsoever**, on either side, and this is what forbids the phantom exit **structurally** rather than by a special case. Membership derived from step 4 — which skips any pair with both ends non-awake — would make a body that **falls asleep inside a trigger** leave the current set without having moved by one ULP. The pass therefore runs at **step 10 bis**: after the proxy update, so poses are final and every proxy is valid including sleepers', and **before** step 11, so a body's membership is established for the tick in which it falls asleep.
- **The price is explicit: sleep does not economise this pass.** A fully resting scene pays it every tick. Nothing forbids an implementation from skipping the work when neither the trigger nor any of its candidates moved — the set is then unchanged by the same argument that lets step 10 skip sleepers — but the observable invariant is the one that is tested: **falling asleep never produces an exit**.
- **Disappearance produces an exit** whatever its cause — separation, removal of the candidate body, removal of the trigger body, shape change, or loss of the sensor role. None of these needs a dedicated path: the full rebuild covers them all, a removed body having no proxy left to visit.
- **Determinism, and M1.1.14 is next.** State and both deltas are deduplicated and sorted by the lexicographic key `(trigger_entity, other_entity)` compared on the **complete entity value** — index **and** generation, never the index alone, two successive occupants of an index having to be distinct and ordered. No hashed container anywhere on this path. The output order is a pure function of the set, independent of tree visit order, worker count and body insertion order, so M1.1.14 **verifies** it instead of having to establish it.

## Out of scope

- **Any emission.** No `TriggerEnter`, no `TriggerExit`, no `EventBus` registration, no `emit`. `forge_3d` does not import `weld_core` and must not start here — C1.1 makes its dependency set an exit metric. The translation is M1.1.15's.
- **The bus-to-Etch bridge.** `World.event_bus` is comptime-typed and the interpreter keeps its own dynamic per-tick `EventStore` because the tree-walker cannot drive the former; no bridge exists between them, so no Etch rule can observe an event produced by Zig today. This is a Tier 0 / Etch problem, it is **not** a physics problem, and M1.1.15 now owns it by name in `engine-phase-1-plan.md`.
- **Any treatment of the `debris` class.** It has no consumer before destruction, and deriving a broad class from an object layer touches no signature, so no freeze imposes a date on it. **Add no `is_debris` field**: debris is a Forge optimisation class, where sensor is a semantic every backend shares.
- **An object-layer matrix.** The engine's object-layer filtering is a mask, here as in the queries. A configurable pair matrix from the project settings stays a separate piece of work with no deadline here.
- **`step()` / `PhysicsWorld` / `PhysicsModule` instantiation / production `BodyType → BroadphaseLayer` wiring / ECS `Transform` sync** — all M1.1.15. Nothing in production inserts a body into the broadphase today; this milestone proves its behaviour through the test harness and must **say so** rather than imply a wiring that does not exist. **The character's presence is inserted by the harness**, exactly as the query suites insert their own proxies.
- **Renaming the `dynamic` broad class.** The name lags its meaning — it is what moves, not what is simulated — and correcting it touches the broadphase and its suites. Noted, not done.
- **Any hysteresis, debounce, dwell time or minimum overlap duration.** §1.13.6 refuses them with an argument; do not reintroduce one under another name because a test flickers. A flickering test at the ULP boundary is measuring the boundary.
- **`TriggerStay`**, in any form, including an internal « still overlapping » list offered as a convenience.
- **`forge_2d`.** `PhysicsModule2D` carries no sensor entry either and `Collider2D.is_sensor` names the same role with a different word; the symmetry is recorded for M1.8.11. Touch nothing under `forge_2d/`.
- Cross-platform determinism (M1.1.14), joints (M1.1.16–18), advanced shapes (M1.1.19–20), vehicle (M1.1.21), the deformable branch (M1.1.22–23).

## Acceptance criteria

### Tests

RED-first on every pinnable behaviour. Every expectation is a **closed form computed in the comment above it**, never a value read back from the implementation. Every suite runs at `f32` and `f64` × Debug and ReleaseSafe. Entry state: **495 tests** in `test-forge-3d`, green at `cbb02b3`.

**Produce the wiring table at the first gate that writes a property.** For every quantity the suite measures: its name, its nature — **delivered guarantee** or **historical metric** — and its treatment. A guarantee is an assertion **per case**; a counter belongs only to a shape allowed to fail. Nine of M1.1.11.1's last twelve findings were in the measuring apparatus rather than the code.

**An absence witness is worth a presence witness.** Half of this milestone's claims are negative — no constraint, no impulse, no wake, no obstruction, no exit. A negative assertion over a configuration that could never have produced the positive proves nothing. Every negative case ships with the **positive control** that proves the apparatus can observe the thing being denied.

**`tests/sensor_test.zig`** (create):

- One trigger, one body entering then leaving: exactly one `enter`, no delta across the maintained ticks, exactly one `exit`. The maintained ticks are asserted as **empty deltas**, not skipped.
- Aggregation: one entity carrying two bodies overlapping the same trigger produces **one** `enter`; removing one of the two produces **no** `exit`; removing the second produces one.
- Reflexive suppression: an entity carrying a trigger body and a solid body that overlap produces nothing at all.
- Orientation: two mutually detecting triggers produce **two** pairs, one per direction. With asymmetric masks, A sees B and B does not see A — asserted in both directions.
- Mask: a candidate on a layer outside the trigger's mask is never detected; the same candidate moved onto a layer inside it is. Same body, same pose, mask alone changed.
- Exactness: a candidate whose AABB overlaps the trigger's while the exact shapes do not is **not** detected. Without this case the suite cannot tell an exact traversal from an AABB one.
- Detection across classes: `static`, `dynamic`, `debris` and another trigger are each detected when the mask allows, and **none** of the four produces a constraint, an impulse or an island. Positive control: the same scene with `is_trigger` cleared does produce them.
- Sleep: a body that falls asleep inside a trigger produces **no** `exit`, and none on any subsequent tick while it stays asleep. Positive control: the same body, woken and moved out, produces one.
- The trigger itself asleep, or static, or never awake: detection is unchanged. A sleeping trigger detects an arriving body.
- Disappearance, four causes, one `exit` each: candidate removed, trigger removed, shape changed out of overlap, `is_trigger` cleared.
- No wake: a body detected by a trigger, and a trigger moved by pose write over a sleeping island, leave the sleepers asleep. Positive control: a real W4 teleport on the same scene wakes them.
- Order: a scene producing several deltas in one tick yields them sorted by complete entity value. Include two entities sharing an index across a generation wrap, deliberately, and assert they are distinct and ordered.
- Rebuild immunity: destroy a detected body, create another that reuses its `BodyId` slot, and assert the new one is not reported as still inside.
- Frozen surface pins: both `BodyDescriptor` fields by name and default; `CollisionShape` at 52 bytes.

**`tests/character_test.zig`** (modify) — non-obstruction, one case per collection path: a trigger straddling the walk path does not stop a sweep, does not appear in depenetration, and is never selected as ground. Each with its positive control — the same volume with `is_trigger` cleared **does** block, **does** depenetrate, **is** selected as ground. Without the controls these three cases pass on a controller that sees nothing at all.

**`tests/broadphase_test.zig`** (modify) — the reversed cells asserted negatively in both directions (`dynamic × trigger` and `trigger × dynamic`), joined to the three that were already `false`. The M1.1.1 assertion this replaces is **deleted**, not commented out.

**`tests/overlap_test.zig` / `tests/raycast_test.zig`** (modify) — a trigger body answers the query entries like any other, and a caller masking its object layer out does not see it. Both directions.

## Notes

**The phantom exit is the way this milestone ships a silent bug, and it has exactly one cause.** Any path that derives membership from something the sleep system filters — step 4's pair walk, the retained candidate set, the moved logs — produces an exit for a body that did not move. It will pass every test written on moving bodies. The pass is independent of the solver for this reason and for no other, and the sleeping-body case is not an edge case in this suite: it is the suite's centre.

**`computePairs` returns a delta, and reading it as a snapshot is the second way.** Two motionless overlapping bodies do not appear in it. A traversal that reuses it will look correct in every test where something moves and be empty in the frame where nothing does. A second call after step 10 does not repair this: proxy updates may repopulate the moved logs, so the result is not reliably empty either — it is simply not the current set. Neither state is a snapshot.

**Never derive engine behaviour from a validation criterion.** C1.1 lists « sensors » in the exit gate; it does not specify the mechanism. The mechanism is `engine-physics-solver.md` §1.13 and nothing else.

**Positive controls, not because tests should be thorough but because half the claims are negative.** « No exit was produced » is satisfied by a pass that produces nothing at all. « A trigger does not block the controller » is satisfied by a controller that collides with nothing. Each negative case is paired with the positive that proves the apparatus can see what is being denied. This is the M1.1.11.1 apparatus-defect class, and it is the dominant one in this milestone by construction.

**The matrix reversal touches a decision, not a value.** M1.1.1 chose `dynamic × trigger = true` and wrote a test asserting it. That choice was reasonable then — nothing consumed the trigger class — and is wrong now. Record it as a revision with its reason; a matrix cell flipped without a trace is indistinguishable from a typo six months later.

**Two entries added to `BodyDescriptor` and zero to `PhysicsModule`.** If the implementation finds itself wanting a function entry — `setBodyTrigger`, `getOverlaps`, anything — that is a STOP and a Claude.ai round-trip, not a decision to take in session. One window remains after this one and it is not this milestone's to spend.

**The `dynamic` broad class holds kinematic bodies, and that will look wrong.** It is what moves, not what is simulated. A reviewer reading `dynamic` as « simulated by the solver » will call the character presence's placement a bug. It is not; the name is.

**Tooling facts established at M1.1.11, M1.1.11.1 and M1.1.12, not to be rediagnosed.** The leak checker's `safety` flag is false by default in ReleaseFast. The pre-commit hook lints only staged files. The build runner's `failed command:` line appears on tests that pass while writing to stderr, and is not a failure. A pipeline's status is its **last** command's: capture `$?` before any filtering and keep the **complete** log on failure. `zsh` does not word-split an unquoted flags variable. Best-of-three does not decide a gap under 5 %: interleave the runs. On a `pull_request` event, `paths-ignore` filters evaluate over **every** file in the PR. `bench.yml` carries `timeout-minutes: 10`, marginal on the Windows runner: a cancellation with no output there is a budget overrun rather than a hang — rerun before suspecting the code. `git checkout <path>` restores from the index and destroys unstaged edits with no prompt — copy to the scratchpad before injecting a counter-factual break, or commit first and revert against the commit.

**The `f64` leg still runs on one machine only.** `-Dphysics_f64` appears nowhere in `.github/`. Every claim this suite makes at `f64` rests on a single target and a single compiler. That is not this milestone's to fix — M1.1.14 owns CI — but a per-precision pin written here carries that standing, and should be worded as a measurement rather than as a property.
