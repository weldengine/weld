# The `unknown` census of the Etch type-checker

Read at `a7a795c2`, `src/etch/types.zig` lines 1–10177, the production half of the file.
Nothing in the tree was changed; the probes ran in a scratch worktree.

## Method

- **The list.** Every production line that mentions `unknown` or `generic`, or calls a
  permissive fit or predicate, found by grep: 307 lines. Seven readers took one range
  each and classified every one of them; they added 89 sites the grep could not see. Seven
  adversarial verifiers re-read every entry, made 55 corrections and added 21 missed
  sites. **417 distinct lines.**
- **A second instrument.** A script found 141 lines that return or bind `unknown`. The
  census classes 140 of them as producers; the 141st is the slot sentinel of
  `reserveOptionalSlots`, rightly no site. The script disagreed with 12 of the census's
  diagnosed / silent verdicts. Read at the code, all 12 go the census's way: eight silent
  paths whose nearby emit sits on another branch, four diagnosed by another pass.
- **Probes.** Twelve silent entries, run as programs: each is accepted with no
  diagnostic, as the census says, beside two controls that fired in the same run. Entry
  8754, run alone, panics as described.

## The jokers that remain

The joker is an `unknown` produced with no diagnostic, which every consumer then lets
through. There are **67 silent producers, in twelve families**; four are unreachable
today, so **63 are reachable**.

**Context producers that are jokers when no slot supplies a type** (7 of the 10):
`none` (7587), `[]` (7937), `[:]` (7977) and `Set.new()` (9092), bound unannotated by a
`let` (6702), and a join whose every branch is `none` (5456, 9838). The other three are
not: an unresolved `.variant` (7584, 8797) is always reported at the end of the check,
and a type parameter (8633) is `.generic` by design.

**The consumers have two roots.** `fitsValue` at 5229 accepts an unknown slot, an
unknown value and a generic slot, and decides the 37 fit calls in the table.
`builtinWhere` at 5352 accepts both for every builtin predicate, and decides 9 more.
The rest are `assertComparable` (8186), `isFloatType` (8194) and inline skips.
`.generic` is accepted by 53 consumers: generic bounds are not tracked.

**The other side of the ledger.** 29 sites refuse because of `unknown`. Some refuse
valid programs the checker cannot represent: a fn parameter `P[]` or a return `-> Self`
is `E0102` (6384, 5772).
